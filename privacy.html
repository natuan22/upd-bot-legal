# UPD Bot — Privacy Policy **Last updated:** October 12, 2026 ## 1. Information We Collect UPD Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, callsigns, personnel roles, server configuration, and MDT case records. ## 2. How We Use Information Information is used to provide bot functionality, manage personnel, maintain case records, and calculate officer performance metrics (KPI). ## 3. Data Storage and Retention Information may be stored in the bot's database for as long as necessary to operate the service or as required by the server's administrative rules. ## 4. Data Sharing Information is not intentionally sold. Access to personnel and case records may be available to authorized server staff as required for server operations. ## 5. Data Deletion Users may contact UPD server administrators to request review or deletion of information, subject to applicable server rules and recordkeeping requirements. ## 6. Security Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. ## 7. Changes This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. ## 8. Contact For privacy questions or data requests, contact the UPD server administrators.
